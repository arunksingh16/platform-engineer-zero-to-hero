9 lessons, about 20 minutes each

Observability from zero

Learn how modern systems are observed — metrics, time series databases, logs, traces, OpenTelemetry and SLOs — one concept at a time, with numbers small enough to work out on paper.

How each lesson works

  1. Idea in plain words with a tiny example.
  2. Play with an animation or interactive widget.
  3. Do it by hand — a short exercise with a hidden answer.
  4. Quiz — pass it to complete the lesson.

Lessons

    Where this takes you

    After lesson 9 you will understand the vocabulary and the maths behind Prometheus, Grafana, Tempo/Jaeger, Loki and OpenTelemetry. That is the foundation for advanced topics: cardinality control, tail-based sampling, exemplars, multi-window burn-rate alerting, eBPF auto-instrumentation and observability cost management.

    Progress is stored only in your browser. No tracking, no dependencies.
